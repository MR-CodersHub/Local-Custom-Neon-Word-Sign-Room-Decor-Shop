Ask Us Anything
How long does production take?
Standard production takes 3–5 business days. Express orders (extra fee) are ready in 1–2 days. All timelines exclude shipping.
Are your neon signs safe to touch?
Yes! Our signs use low-voltage LED neon flex that stays cool to the touch, making them safe for children's rooms, pets and high-traffic areas.
Can I use outdoor?
Our Commercial Business range includes IP65-rated waterproof outdoor neon signs. Standard home signs are indoor only.
What's the lifespan of a NeonLux sign?
Our LED neon signs last 50,000+ hours, which is over 17 years of continuous use at 8 hours per day. They're built to last a lifetime.
Do you offer returns?
Because each sign is custom-made, we don't accept returns. However, if there's a defect or damage in transit, we replace it immediately at no cost.
