Our Process

From Order to Glow

1. You Submit Your Design

Tell us your text, font preference, color, size and backing color. Use our online form or WhatsApp us directly.

Takes: 5 minutes
2. Digital Proof Sent

Our designer creates a digital mockup of your sign within 24 hours. You approve or request changes — unlimited revisions.

Within: 24 hours
3. Crafting Begins

Once approved, our artisans handcraft your sign from premium LED neon flex. Quality-checked at every step of the build.

Production: 3–5 days
4. Quality Inspection

Every sign undergoes our 12-point quality check: glow uniformity, power test, backing finish, cable management and more.

Takes: Same day

Takes: 1 day
