How do I place a custom order?
Simply fill in our contact form above with your text, color, size and service type. We'll create a free digital proof within 24 hours for your approval before any payment is taken.
How long does production take?
After you approve your digital proof, production typically takes 3–5 business days. Express orders (available for an extra fee) can be ready in 1–2 days. Shipping adds 1–3 business days on top.
Do you offer international shipping?
Yes! We ship to 25+ countries worldwide. UK shipping is free on orders over £100. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and sign size.
Can I see my design before I pay?
Absolutely! We provide a full digital proof (rendered mockup in your chosen space) before any payment. You can request unlimited changes until you're 100% happy, then we'll proceed with payment.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, and bank transfers. For business orders, invoice with 30-day net terms is available.
What if my sign arrives damaged?
In the rare event of transit damage, simply email us a photo within 48 hours of delivery. We'll replace your sign completely free of charge, no questions asked, and arrange collection of the damaged item.
