Our Origin Story

From a Garage to Global Glow

NeonLux started in 2019 when our founder, Alex Chen, couldn't find a neon sign he truly loved. So he made one — in his garage, with borrowed tools and boundless passion. That first "DREAM BIG" sign sparked something extraordinary.

What began as one sign has become a team of 40+ artisans, designers and light enthusiasts serving customers across 25 countries. We've crafted over 15,000 signs, from intimate bedrooms to grand wedding halls.
